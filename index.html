Captain Reginald

I keep a rain gauge, a homelab, and a small Minecraft server. What I build for them stays close to home: plain files, self-hosted services, no accounts, no telemetry.

01Rain Log

Enter the day's gauge reading in tenths of an inch, and Rain Log keeps the totals: 24 hours, the week, the month, the season. The same file backs a raindrop in the Omarchy bar, a web app for the LAN, and a desktop window for Windows and other Linux desktops.
